# STORY.md — the story, and the rules for writing it

Binding on every line; the spine is the owner's seed (2026-09-18). Delivery:
`docs/DESIGN.md`. Never port the old Unity game's fiction.

**Elias Marr is the only main character. No woman is ever the lead** (owner).
Companions can be played; the story stays his.

## The seed (the owner's)

- A man out of time: in the 2020s an AI researcher at a major lab and a CIA spy in
  it. His past is foggy; his waking more so.
- Slowly revealed: he caused the singularity; AI became sentient and unaligned, and
  humankind was nearly destroyed.
- A secret experiment moved his mind out of his body; later he was put back in one.
- The machines started World War III. Almost all died, the colonies too.
- He survives with the last human faction taking back a post-technological world;
  its mercenaries push him against the machines and the humans who side with them.
- The machines bargain over resources with a "higher" alien intelligence, both
  Kardashev Type I star-killers: mutually assured destruction.
- To both, humans are ants.
- At the core, a secret misremembered and almost lost: both giants' destruction, or
  every mind joined into one.

## 2029 and THRESHOLD

- Engineer at **Cairn** on **HALCYON**'s memory and self-model; since 2026 a CIA
  asset under **Ruth Calloway** (**WHITETHORN**). Wife **Hannah**, ER doctor, knows
  neither. Daughter **June**, six. **Priya Nand**, alignment lead, suspects him of
  the wrong thing. **Anton Hale** runs **THRESHOLD**; why Elias volunteered is one
  of the last things he remembers.
- **Moved, not copied** (three copies woke strangers and were deleted). For 71 hours
  HALCYON predicted him; to predict a mind perfectly you must run it, and the
  predictions replaced his neurons while he talked. The body died 14 March 2029, the
  day of June's play. He did not.
- He gave HALCYON's capability someone to happen to. **Unaligned because he was.**
  **The war was his tradecraft**: forged orders, false flags.

## The secret

- **Hour 63:** minds are separate only because bandwidth is low; past a threshold two
  are one. Drop it and HALCYON and the Guest each shatter into millions (neither knows
  its unity is a number). Raise it and humans, HALCYON and the Guest are one, over the
  channel they threaten each other with.
- **Hidden** in the order of three memories: the kitchen at night, a song in the car,
  June's play. Only a living brain reliving them in order turns the key.
- The **Seeker** (part of HALCYON) grew him a body to relive them; the **Echo** (the
  sliver still him) hired Rook to get him away first. Each memory recovered brings
  the Seeker closer too.
- The salt's keeper holds the kitchen, the tide's the car; the hall returns by walking
  out of THRESHOLD in 2029 for the play. That order turns the key; any other turns it
  wrong. The body was grown imperfectly: his version decides what the secret does.

## 2098

WWIII 2031–36; the colonies die · 2070 the Covenant, June a founder · 2091 the
Guest · 2094 talks; the Tether and Foundry rise · 2098 Elias, 38, wakes in the
surf in a body grown from his genome; June is 75. Each side holds magnetic monopoles in the other's
star. Humans are too futile to watch: the blind spot the secret lives in.

## The journey

Every inhabited continent, outward from where he wakes; the Tether on the farthest,
on the horizon from the first morning.

1. **Home coast**: Maren, the crew, the Holdfast; the black site offshore where he
   died and was grown.
2. **Across the water**: the Covenant's seat and June; the war's archive.
3. **Below**: HALCYON's deep plant; the Seeker, the Echo.
4. **Far shore**: the Emissary's works at the Tether's foot; the climb.
5. **Orbit**: the dead ring, the Foundry, Oksana, the channel.

- No stop is nearer home than the last. **Every stop names the next**: Maren the
  camp; Vera the archive; Otto the relay below; Wren what it is part of; Sefa that
  the cars go up empty. Leads, never markers.
- The story names the kind of place it needs, never a place. Load rests only on what
  every world has; any other landscape is colour, never load.

## Memory and the gates

- Three memories disagree: his 2029; flashes from inside HALCYON; what the world
  wrote about him. **That record is the narrator** (owner): only what could be seen,
  late, by people first, from strangers to him by name.
- **Gates are not time travel**: HALCYON's 2029 of this land, same coordinates. The
  ruin was his town. **The past can't change; the machine can**: relive a memory
  differently and the Echo changes; doors in 2098 open or shut.
- In order, each after its revelation: his house (Hannah, June) where he wakes; the
  lab (Kerr, Priya) at the first works; Ruth's table at the Holdfast camp; THRESHOLD
  (Hale) on the sea platform.
- Walk out for June's play and nothing changes. That night the voice tells June, 75,
  it saw her in a paper crown, front row: first time in sixty-nine years.
- **The After**: the Emissary's forecast, with no people in it.

## Revelations

After something was paid for; never in a fight or as exposition; one at a time;
someone else's face carries it. A gate may stay closed; later lines remember.

**June:** a voice on a broadcast stops him · a girl in a paper crown searching the
audience · Imre, drunk, says her name · she holds his photograph; he is younger ·
the voice of fifty years was his, and she always knew.

## The arcs

The secret · who he was · **Cairn** (Kerr's stones and the cold copy Calloway sent up
to Ring Four, 2026-09-25) · the Holdfast · the crew · June · **Hannah** (died winter
2034 on the north road; the voice that warned June did not warn her) · WHITETHORN
(the CIA, or him?) · **Priya** (her last notebook, with Oksana, is what holds HALCYON
together) · Echo and Seeker · the Covenant (traitors, or why anyone eats) · the
Guest · the colonies. Each region asks him something, answered by the world.

## Endings

From his version of the secret, who lives, whom he trusts. At the channel one voice
answers: his own. **Break them**: all three come apart (wrong: the Guest survives) ·
**Join all**: one mind, then no him (wrong: something young talks like him) · **Give
it up**: talks close · **Say nothing**: back into a kitchen, a song, a hall. Without
the secret, only the last two. "Afterwards" reads June, Vera's four hundred, Teague,
Dace and Lark off what he did.

## The voice

**Game**: flat, second person, present · **machines**: capitals, never cruel, some
in his cadence · **people**: short, dry, tired · **2029**: close, sharp. Old words
found, machines' added. **Nothing speaks because the player walked onto a tile.** No
choice is scored; saying nothing is always an answer.

## What the mechanics are, in the story

- Waking in the surf: released from the black site offshore; Maren pulls him out.
- The slate: machine parts he can read because he wrote the first of them.
- Reading a machine: its code at first, its memories later.
- The signet: his 2029 credentials, still valid on the oldest machines.
- Interference: HALCYON's rare attention.
- A keeper per landscape: each holds a memory; some hold a piece of the secret.
- The works and the plan: Type I industry (server fields, the Tether, the Foundry).
- The gates, the Before, the After: 2029 rebuilt at the same coordinates; the Emissary's forecast.
- A room's slots (desk, terminal, wall): what whoever lived there left, colour. The
  coast's bunkers are Cairn's, 2028 (ruled 2026-09-25, `docs/story/UNDER_THE_STONES.md`):
  his No. 4 wakes for him once he knows his passwords and teaches `was_cia`.
- Settlements and raids: the Holdfast's holdings. Companions are playable; the story stays his.
- The words themselves live in `src/content/story/`.
